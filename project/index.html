OVERVIEW

Invoicing, made simpler.

InvoiceFlow is a mobile invoice management application designed to simplify creating, organizing, and managing invoices for businesses and independent professionals.

Built with Flutter, the app focuses on a clean, intuitive experience and a structured workflow for essential business operations. It helps users manage invoices digitally and stay organized with less reliance on manual processes.

Through this project, I explored mobile application development, UI/UX design principles, and the process of turning a business idea into a complete digital product.

PROJECT FOCUS

  • Mobile application
  • Flutter
  • Invoice management
  • Business workflow
  • UI/UX design

OVERVIEW

Run every part of your gym in one place.

PowerGym is a complete gym management platform designed to help fitness businesses organize and manage their daily operations through a centralized digital system.

The platform replaces manual processes with tools for managing members, subscriptions, payments, coaches, schedules, expenses, and business analytics. Dashboards, structured data management, financial tracking, and automated workflows help gym owners run operations more efficiently.

Through PowerGym, I explored building a real-world SaaS-style application, focusing on scalability, clean architecture, and creating a product that solves practical business challenges.

PLATFORM FEATURES

  • Member and subscription management
  • Payments and financial tracking
  • Coaches and schedules
  • Expenses and analytics
  • Dashboards and workflows
  • Scalable SaaS-style architecture
